Nazarick Albedo

A private, self-hosted personal assistant. Not a public service.

Nazarick Albedo is a personal assistant that runs entirely on hardware owned by its single user. It is not offered to the public, has no other users and is not sold or distributed.

What it does with Google data

  • Gmail: reads messages of the owner's own accounts on request (read-only).
  • Google Calendar: reads events and, only after explicit confirmation by the owner, creates or changes events.
  • Google Drive: reads files of the owner's own accounts on request (read-only).

Data is processed only on the owner's own computers and is never sent to any third party. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
